Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

What areas do you serve?
We proudly provide our top-tier cleaning services to all of Chicago and its surrounding suburbs. If you are unsure if you fall within our radius, please reach out!
Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?
No, we bring our own high-quality, professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment. If you have specific products you prefer us to use, just let us know.
Are you insured?
Yes, absolutely. We are fully licensed and insured to give you complete peace of mind while we care for your property.
How does the estimation process work?
Simply fill out our online form with details about your space. We'll review the square footage, room count, and service type, and get back to you promptly with a transparent, free estimate.
Do I have to be home during the cleaning?
It's entirely up to you! Many of our clients provide us with a key or access code so they can return to a sparkling clean home. We ensure absolute security and respect for your property.
How do I pay for the service?
We accept various forms of payment including credit/debit cards, Zelle, and checks. Payment is typically due upon completion of the cleaning service.
What if I am not satisfied with the clean?
We offer a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with any part of our service, please contact us within 24 hours, and we will return to re-clean the specific area at no additional cost.
Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?
Yes, we understand that schedules change. We kindly request at least 48 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling to avoid a cancellation fee.
